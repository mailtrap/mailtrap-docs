# Tabular

Upload Tabular email templates to Mailtrap and use them for email sending.

<a href="https://tabular.email/integrations/mailtrap" class="button primary">Tabular documentation</a>

### Overview

Create, update, and manage your email designs in Tabular. Then upload them to Mailtrap with one click and send them with high deliverability.

#### Prerequisites

Before we start, you'll need to:

* [Verify your sending domain](https://docs.mailtrap.io/general/sending-domain)
* [Create and save an API token](https://docs.mailtrap.io/general/api-tokens)

### Connect Tabular to Mailtrap

1. In Tabular, open the email you want to use with Mailtrap and click **Use email**.
2. Click **Integrations**, choose **Mailtrap**, and name the authorization.
3. Enter a template category, paste your API token, and click **Add authorization**.
4. Click **Connect to** and choose **templates** from the dropdown.
5. When the connection works, it appears under **Your email is currently connected to…**

The email appears in Mailtrap under **Templates**. To upload a new version of the email, click **Upload email** in the same list in Tabular.

To send the template, use its UUID from the template's **Integration** tab. Read more about [email templates](https://docs.mailtrap.io/general/email-templates).

### Use cases

* Send Tabular email templates with Mailtrap
* Test Tabular email templates with Mailtrap [Email Sandbox](https://docs.mailtrap.io/getting-started/email-sandbox)

If you need help with your Tabular integration, contact our support team at **support@mailtrap.io**.
