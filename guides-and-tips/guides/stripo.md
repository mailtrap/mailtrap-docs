# Stripo

Export Stripo email templates to Mailtrap and send emails with those templates.

<a href="https://support.stripo.email/en/articles/11431744-how-to-export-an-email-template-to-mailtrap" class="button primary">Stripo documentation</a>

### Overview

Create mobile-responsive emails with Stripo's drag-and-drop builder. When your template is ready, export it to Mailtrap and send it through the Mailtrap Email API, whether it's a campaign, a transactional email, or a bulk update.

#### Prerequisites

Before we start, you'll need to:

* [Verify your sending domain](https://docs.mailtrap.io/general/sending-domain)
* [Create and save an API token](https://docs.mailtrap.io/general/api-tokens)

### Export a Stripo template to Mailtrap

1. In Stripo, open the email template you want to use, click **Export**, and choose **Mailtrap**.
2. Enter an **Export account name** and your Mailtrap API token, add the email **Category**, and click **Export**.
3. In Mailtrap, open **Templates**. Your Stripo template appears in the list.

To send the template, use its UUID from the template's **Integration** tab. Read more about [email templates](https://docs.mailtrap.io/general/email-templates).

### Use cases

* Send Stripo email templates with Mailtrap
* Test Stripo email templates with Mailtrap [Email Sandbox](https://docs.mailtrap.io/getting-started/email-sandbox)

If you need help with your Stripo integration, contact our support team at **support@mailtrap.io**.
